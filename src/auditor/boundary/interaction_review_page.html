Relation definitions

Continue: the same episode continues through motion cycles, pauses or regrasping.

Same action, new instance: an episode ends and the same action begins anew. Releasing the object is not required.

New action: a different immediate goal or outcome begins.

Unobservable: the context does not establish the relation.

Release and continuous contact are independent evidence, not boundary rules. One-sided starts and ends are recorded separately.
