## What a closure does and does not mean
Neither branch is closed because the idea is wrong. Both are closed because THESE signals -- global Qwen side vectors, local hand-crop vectors, detection reliability -- do not support them at a deployable operating point. Reopening either requires a new observable, not another threshold over the same scores.

The consequence for what gets built next is direct: no further threshold search, no further fusion of the existing scores, and no held-out evaluation until a single primary policy exists that was selected on the deployed metric.
